The **Mahishasura Mardini Stotram** (also called the *Ai Giri Nandini* stotram) is a 21-verse hymn from the Markandeya Purana, praising the Goddess Durga in her form as slayer of the demon Mahishasura.

Each verse carries four phrases, 84 phrases in total across the hymn. Every verse is unpacked here: the Sanskrit, a transliteration, the word-by-word meaning, and the mythological and spiritual reading behind it.
