It’s day two of Navratri. If you managed to take action, doesn't matter at what scale, if you were successful in just starting the action, you have done your part on day one, well done.

But this is where things might get more discomforting. Some of you might already be taking action but not sticking enough. That is ok, and there are valid reasons for it. Today's modern world has made human mind so receptive to everything that it has lost the ability to focus. The social media, the wrath of the internet, the gigantic pile of content on the internet is so overwhelming that the temptation to indulge in those media is just incomparable and not apt for the capacity of our minds. That is what this next step, day two of Navratri, is meant for. To focus and ignore the things that flicker a fire of attachment and desire in your mind.

Stop scrolling right now!

Just for a moment, after reading this paragraph, act immediately:

- Turn off the notification, or snooze the phone or laptop for 15 minutes minimum

- Close your eyes for a minute and let yourself be in the moment

- Just don't do anything, just for a moment, let every thought go through you, your mind, don't overthink, don't panic, just let it go

Just start doing it.

I have my phone always on silent mode, so I flipped it downwards to prevent me from being distracted while writing this.

The biggest hurdle of modern life is not inaction after taking action; it then becomes the root cause, which is the discomfort of feeling incompetent to do the thing. Embrace it and sit with it, that is a truth that you have to accept, and without attention to it, it can't be changed like a miracle.

The focus of day two is to burn your mind with the discomfort, let the war begin, let it question you, threaten you, persuade you, even contemplate you, but don't follow the mind, just listen and be still, let it go.

The focus of day two is also about building up the discipline after taking the first step. Its about taking the first step again. This time, the mind might overwhelm you, but be patient, be focused, just be.

The focus of day two is most importantly about detaching yourself from the ego. Self from the ego part, live like it doesn't matter if your actions lead to your goal or not, the focus is on the process and not on the fruit of it.

So, let's embrace discomfort and relate to the mythology of Maa Brahmacharini on the second day of Navratri.

## Maa Brahmacharini

As we transcend into the second day, we continue to embrace the journey of Maa Parvati, who decided to perform intense penance to marry Lord Shiva, and attain her higher self. She went to the higher mountains, into the caves of Himalaya to perform her penance.

This form of Maa Parvati or Maa Shailputri from day one is known as Maa Brahmacharini. She is one of the nine forms (navdurga) of Maa Parvati, which is described as the one performing intense penance and undertaking the route of celibacy, detachment from the materialistic world.

It is believed that during the period of intense penance, she feed herself just leaves, she gave up on food and water. With the intense focus, she was able to sustain and was able to transcend into the higher subconscious realm. That is why this form of Brahmacharini represents the power of spiritual focus and dedication. It also implores the qualities of self-discipline and detachment from the worldly desires.

It is through her penance and the process of meditation, she was able to recollect her previous birth as Sati, and realize her true powers and self. By removing one's ego from self, we are able to realize the power and true self, that is the message this story speaks loudly at least to me.

## Removing Ego from Self

By focus and discipline, she controlled her mind from tangled thoughts and controlled her emotions in the end. This shows the power of letting your mind embrace discomfort for a while, but in the long run helping yourself.

The only thing that would keep yourself from doing the action apart from will, will be you ego, the mind sees ego as his friend as uses it to ensnare you in his wrath.

What is ego?

- I want to be well-known writer

- I want to earn fame

- I want to earn money

- I want to become a better person

The theme here is I and want.

What self says:

- How can I be a well-known author

- How can I earn fame

- How can I money

- How can I become a better person

Can you see the difference?

The question is the same, but when people attach themselves to the result that is when the ego part comes into the plot. I am not saying to stop earning money, stop pursuing your goals, no, do it, but be aware don't attach to the goal itself, the goal is not the purpose, the process the path, the direction is.

## Focus on sounds, not thoughts

Maa Brahmacharini was able to attain celibacy and also peace within, that is a core reason why she was able to ascend into the higher subconscious self. This you might call a myth, but can you underestimate the power of spiritual energy? How can one survive with only leaves?

This is a lesson that Maa Brahmacharini teaches us about focus. Through her intense penance, the act of meditation, the act of channeling one's energy to detach from the desires, she realized her true self, her divine form of goddess. She was one with the nature, she focused on the surrounding, the things that are just there, this shows how you can control your thoughts, the mind might make it harder for you to think that thoughts just can't be controlled, but it can be.

Initially, you will be bamboozled with questions and thoughts that your mind might throw at you, you might be even tempted to listen, but you chose to act regardless. That was the first step, but mind might employ more nasty questions and thoughts that might question your existence, the existential crisis? It puts you into anxious cycles of misery. Don't let it! Just listen to nature, the sound around you, or within you, the breathe, the bird chirps, the flow of water, the subtle consistent sound of fan, some random person talking on the phone, some caw of crow, the beeps of cars and vehicles, the things that happen and for no reason, they exist, yet we never noticed them.

Listen to the voice of nature, let it channel you, let it escape you from the trap that your mind has crafted for you. Know your potential yet don't get carried away, know that you are able to lift yourself from the situation you are in, that is what is penance, to focus on the things you control, not to chase the uncontrollable.

You cannot control

- your death

- words after you spoke

- words entering your ears

- the path, the destiny, the fruit

You can control

- your breathe (until you are alive)

- the words you let out

- the words you react to when enters your ears

- the steps you take knowing the path

Focus on the controllable, don't let mind dissuade you from the path of your action.

> Imagine starting out a trek to a mountain, initially it feels like a hurdle to climb, but we slowly start enjoying the nature and it cradles us from the hard parts, we might be driven from the iconic view at the top, but we truly continue because we are listening to nature, and it motivates us to keep seeing, keep climbing.

Nature is your best friend, she is mother nature of course. Whenever I feel overwhelmed, I just close my eyes and listen to what it has to say, it gives me clarity and helps me slow down my mind rambling thoughts.

## Meaning from the Myths

Maa Brahmacharini is a true portrayal of the power of focus and spiritual energy, she guides her focus to ascend into her higher self, detaching herself from her ego and desires. In the modern world, people think that these words of celibacy and higher spiritual realm are no longer relevant; however it is not about words or terms, its about your self-liberating from that ego. The ego and limited knowledge that keeps you in your inaction-spiral.

Let go of your thoughts, your negative and blocking believes, let go of everything, just listen to what Mother Nature has to say, listen not to speak, listen to understand, conserve the energy, channel your focus for the betterment of your thoughts and others.

This is the message that we can take and break free from the trap that the mind has been spiraling us into through the ways, powers and life of Maa Brahmacharini.
