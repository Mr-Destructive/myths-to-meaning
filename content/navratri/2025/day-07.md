It is day seven now, and we have crossed many inner battles. We have faced discomfort, owned our past, stayed devoted, shown kindness, detached from desires, and slain inner demons. Today is about confronting fear itself, the darkness that lurks, the unknown, the shadows inside us.

Day seven of Navratri honours the fierce and protective form of Maa Durga, Maa Kaalratri, the destroyer of darkness and fear.

## Maa Kaalratri

Maa Kaalratri is one of the fiercest avatars of the Divine Mother.

Her name, “Kaalratri,” means “night of time/death”, she ends darkness, ignorance, and fear.

During the war against demons, Shumbha and Nishumbha, when gods and goddesses struggled, the fierce form Kaalratri emerged from the forehead of the Goddess to kill the demon generals Chanda and Munda.

She also confronted Raktabeej, whose blood drops would spawn new demons. Kaalratri drank his blood before it touched the ground, preventing clones from forming, and destroyed him.

Thus, as per the myths and culture, she is the divine warrior who destroys fear, ignorance, darkness, evil, not just outside, but in the hidden realms of the mind.

## Acknowledge the darkness

This is a theme we have seen in the past lessons too. In order to overcome anything, you need a humble acknowledgment of its existing within you. You need to be humble enough to accept that it resides within you, it could be a negative or positive virtue.

The demon Raktabeej, darkness, fear, and hidden threats symbolise what lies unseen. Kaalratri does not shy from darkness, she enters it. We must face our fears, shadows, the parts of ourselves we hide. If we deny them, they grow in darkness.

In quiet moments, ask: “What scares me?” “What shame or doubt do I avoid?” Name them. Bring light to hidden worries by writing them, speaking them. Letting it out helps to avoid the lonely race your mind’s tricks you in playing, after those things are out, you feel the reality, hopes emerge, solutions start to form, and it starts to get easier with each attempt.

## Transform fear into protection wisely

Kaalratri protects devotees from harm, darkness, and ignorance. Her weapons and her blessings together show her force is not mindless violence but purposeful.

Inner strength needs direction. Without wisdom, it can become anger or harm. What scares you can also protect you. Fear can alert you to boundary violations, dangers, and growth edges.

When strong emotion arises, pause. Decide whether to act or wait. Channel energy into meaningful work, not destructive outbursts. Learn to listen to fear. Ask: “Is this fear protective or limiting?” Use its signal, don’t be controlled by it. Build capacity so fear becomes a guide, not a jailer.

### Stand for light when darkness threatens

She is the destroyer of darkness. After she acts, light is restored. In the world, when negativity, injustice, and harmful trends spread, we must stand firm.

Use your clarity and strength to support others. Speak against injustice, uplift the weak, be a light in dark times.

## Meaning from the Myths

Maa Kaalratri shows us that the first step to freedom is to face our fears. Fear and shame grow in silence, so when we bring them to light, they lose their grip. In our own lives, this means being honest about what scares us, what we avoid, and what parts of ourselves we try to hide. Naming them is where real courage begins.

Her fierce form also reminds us that courage is not wild or uncontrolled. She is powerful, yet precise. She waits for the right moment and then strikes. This teaches us that courage means choosing wisely, not just reacting. We too can pause, breathe, and then act with purpose. Directing our energy toward the right actions helps us solve problems without hurting ourselves or others.

Finally, Maa Kaalratri restores balance and brings peace after the battle. Her story teaches that facing darkness is not only about fighting but also about creating light. We can do the same in daily life. When we stay calm in hard times, speak for what is right, and support those who struggle, we become a source of light for others. Our courage has the power to guide and heal.

May Maa Kaalratri give you the courage and strength required for recognizing and fighting the inner and outer demons.
