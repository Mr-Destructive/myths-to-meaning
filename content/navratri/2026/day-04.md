After walking around the uncertainty and rising from the trap of fear and ego, we can start the process of creation. I mean all of actions are a process of creation right? But are they responsible creations? Like do we create something and leave it as is? or do we nurture it so that it blooms in its full capacity and original intent. There is I guess a difference in sheer creation and a responsible sustained creation. One is superficial, the other teaches you about the process, creates unbound joy and has more responsibility attached to it, since its more powerful.

## Introduction

I am a software developer, and you know what comes with it by default? Endless side projects. We call it side-project graveyard. We create and abandon it immediately after its creation. That’s why we are software developers, and not software maintainers. Open source maintainers are the real developers who create and work on it till its usable and maintain it long term.

## Creation for the sake of it

The software developers are mostly creating for the sake of it (ok, don’t get me wrong, creating is also hard, sometimes there needs to be people who’ll do that hard work for you, but now we have AI). I don’t want to bring AI here. I’ll leave that as is. But the point is, creating is good. Even if you are doing it just to create it, or to bring it into existence, it requires a good amount of thought work and effort to start the process of creation. Remember if you are creating then I think you are ahead of 60% of the people atleast who just think about creating.

But to get to the 90% you need to go beyond the creation. You need to have the broader picture and think beyond the creation process.

## Creating with responsibility

Creating is one step, we think of it as the final step. But I don’t think its quite the final step, in fact its a first step in understanding what have we created or what are we presented with. And then moving towards making it perfect(well don’t attach perfectionism here) just improving and making the thing with whatever best effort you can put on. Sustaining the creation is what I am talking about. In the age of AI slop, anyone can create, but can someone create with care and responsibility, after the thing has been created, can someone put the last mile effort in making it better.

If you are not getting what I am saying, then simply remember your mother. She has not just given birth to you, doing that itself is not trivial, its just crazy to just think for man how can one human stay sane when there is another life being created in her body. Just crazy amount of hardships a women has to face, but she doesn’t stop there, she goes beyond it and nurtures and makes the child grow till her death. That is responsible creation at its best. I mean nothing compares to what mother does. Truly, no words for that.

I am not saying to that, nor I or any human will be able to match the kindness of mother, but just a 1% of her can take us to amazing places and opurtunities I feel.

## Conclusion

On the fourth day of Navratri, its Maa Kushmanda who we worship, with the message that creation and sustaenance of creation are two different things. To be able to do both is kindness and consistency at its best. There is deeper meaning in interpretations I did [last year](https://mythstomeaning.substack.com/p/navratri-day-4-kindness-is-strength) and the [year before](https://mythstomeaning.substack.com/p/navratri-day-4-2024-maa-kushmanda) it, so I just keep finding different meaning to the same principles.

I think I learnt from writing this that, no project is dead, I can still work on it. I can care. Because no one will for me.
