Writing at this time of year has become almost a tradition for me, doing it for the last 2 years and now back at it. I agree, from last year till today I never touched this blog. I can have excuses, but I’ll just give one reason, I am not perfect, I failed to write here. But believe it or, from October - December 2025, I completed my novel. Atleast one of the rough drafts of it. I was happy. But then, nothing…

I don’t want to drag AI here, but I feel like no one will read or value my writing in the age of AI. I think I am being too harsh here, or even impractical. But my mind played tricks on me, and I was convinced till today that writing would not be useful. Today, I am asking, “From when was writing a useful tool? It was an act of self-pleasure, self-indulgence, right?” I have been living in a cave, it seems. If people get value out of my writing, that is just a by-product, but the main point is to discipline your thoughts.

Enough of the reasons and excuses. I am here to celebrate Navratri in my own way. I will write and share what I have learnt or should in order to defeat the demon within me, the Mahishasura, the Ravana within me.

I am not perfect, nobody is.

I have flaws that my inner self knows, yet the mind always shuns it and keeps on feeding that voice, instead of me taking action against it. This post is an act towards shunning that voice and filling in with positivity.

## Introduction

Navratri, I won’t explain what Navratri is, right? Well, actually, I find the definition changing each year subtly, getting deeper, more nuanced, more personal, and more exciting. Yes, it’s about defeating or overcoming the inner demon, the mind. It’s not rejecting it, its accepting and choosing not to let all the thoughts become our actions. Our mind throws 1000s of thoughts at us, not all of them are good, we should be able to sift and act only on the good ones. The “good” depends on context, for nothing is “good” or “bad” in this world, right?

This year, though, having seen my flaws more powerfully, I want to confront them. I want to defeat the inner Mahishasur within me. I know its there, but I keep on delaying the confrontation. The biggest one is “after a win, we stop questioning the story we tell about ourselves”.

There is that voice that shows up right after I finish the action. Not while I’m struggling with it, when I’m struggling I’m questions, I am curious, I have something to push against. The voice shows up after, when the action is done and I no longer look at it, rather see it as a finished product, as a artifact of my hard work. And the annoying part is that it never sounds like a flaw. It sounds like pride, like I’m just being mature about things. Thats exactly why I never confronted it.

And I think I am realizing a version of the Mahishasura in me, in a place I didn’t expect. Not in the anger or pride, but in his or my victory. In my fulfilled actions and deeds. Its different from the attachment of work, here the work is left behind and thumped like a mark of victory.

## When the success makes you blind

I read the story of Mahishasura at some time, it was this [pdf](https://devimahatmya.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/DEVI-MAHATMYA-FULL.pdf). In this, Mahishasura has taken over the heaven after defeating the gods. When Maa Durga/Devi/Chandika/among the hundred names, decided to attack him, he was ignorant about it. That part is different but what he did after being the king, i.e. after having the success, or completing his actions, was to force attack. He won over the heaven with the same method, so he didn’t question who was attacking him this time, he only knew one method and kept believing and using it thereafter without questioning. That is what blind success is.

I think its what winning does. A victory or success teaches me a way of doing or a method, and slowly that method becomes the only thing you can practise and see. And that’s the scary part because from the inside none of this feels like arrogance, it feels like experience. I have written 80k words, my mind starts to link it with a form of experience without validating or questioning what those 80k words, or any form of action are, what the intent is.

I didn’t question or validate that story. I didn’t ask a single person. I just believed it, and I kept believing it for months, because believing it was easier than finding out if the story was good or not.

I’m not saying the worry about AI is nothing, maybe its genuine. But it was never a question I was looking into, it was a excuse or a verdict I was living under. That is the difference, and that is the part I want to change. This is the part, I am realizing it as I write. Just truly grateful and more validating that this period of time is just true divine grace to humans.

## Curiosity and humility breaks the blind success

I think I have stopped becoming more curious, I didn’t ask myself, why would one read my story, why would I read mine, will I find the thing I wanted to tell if this story existed before my younger self? I was not humble enough to accept that it was a made up story, it was half baked, it was written on the fly, and that is ok as well, not saying its wrong, but accepting that with a humility and a sense of improving was lacking.

This is the part that makes Mahishasura, a asura right? A buffalo demon, animal instinct, no questioning or humblenss.

This year I am trying to question this piece as a critique. It’s not a great post, its like sharing my personal struggle, some guessing in the air, but most of it is true. I am struggling that I accept, the changes are rapid that is know, but I cannot deny that I have stopped writing just because there is no validation form people.

This also ties with the principles of Maa Shailputri, which I have written about it to some length in [2025](https://mythstomeaning.substack.com/p/navratri-2025-day-1) and [2024](https://mythstomeaning.substack.com/p/navratri-day-1-2024-worshiping-maa-shailputri) too. Its about accepting that we are flawed and living with that discomfort for a while. It helps to make us thrive for being a better human.

## Conclusion

I think I will attempt to write again, with curiosity and humility. That might lead to more satisfied life it seems that remorsing and feeling dread of lack of actions all the time.

This is just the first day, I want to sustainably write not just for the next 9 days, but for months and years to come. I hope you find and confront your inner demon too and accept it and take actions. Till then, best wishes for your life.

Happy Navratri everyone, enjoy your garba and tune into the devotional songs!
