We are at a stage that we know we have done something and its not working out. For me its my novel, I have written the first draft, but not sure what to do next, edit it or begin new. The ego part of me wants to publish it, nothing wrong with that, but my fear side is telling me to write a new story. Both are confusing and leading to no action. If I start a new, my mind will always think about the 1st one, what if it was good? What if ….! But even editing the existing work feels like dread. I want something new is what my mind calls, and in the end, its neither, procrastination wins, fear and ego killed the ambition. Uncertainty is the thing that bewilders a calm mind and even good intents. Uncertainty is not bad, in fact is the way of nature, but our mind wants the path of least resistance.

Do we have the third choice?

Maybe we do.

## Introduction

Today is the third day, I am a bit disappointed in myself, a built guilt a bit fear and anxiety. I haven’t written a novel that I want to really write, there is just one. Like I could have explained the novel in a short story, but nothing.

Today after knowing I have completed my actions almost a year ago, I decided to think about a new novel. And that thought leaves me with another uncomfortable question, have I been working towards the wrong story, or am I simply afraid to do the work that comes after finishing?

## Step back, accept and think aloud

But is there a way out apart from those 2 paths. I think I can see another way here. There always is. I think I want to explore my 1st novel in such a way that drives the curiosity again, maybe a short story about some side character, because once I write the story, editing feels dreadful. I know I have to get skilled at both writing and editing but its another level of discomfort, I don’t want to make things harder for me. If just maybe I write a short story, it might bring up some motivation or some part of the story that I have neglected, something might happen, again, there is uncertainty but its driven by curiosity and learning something. As opposed to fear or anxiety.

Maybe sometimes we can’t face the reality due to fears or anxiety or even ego. We can’t avoid it, at some point we’ll have to push through it. Though facing it with intensity or force might not be always ideal. Think about it. Mahishasura also tried to stop Maa Durga when she started to attack the demons, he realised “Oh, I being attacked, there was a flaw in my plan, I need to attack with force then”. Attacking with force is not bad, but blind force is not going to get you far. Embracing the discomfort with stable mind is important and not taking rash decisions, the fear and ego might be there for doing just that, clouding your judgement. I think we don’t have to attach the outcomes to our actions, we have to accept it has happened and work around it, understand the problem, don’t go deep directly, look around, the problem might be narrow then your estimate. It might be deeper, but its worth taking a high level look around.

Sometimes, we think the only way to overcome a problem is to confront it with greater force. But force without understanding can make things worse right?

Courage doesn’t always mean charging straight into a battle. Sometimes, it means keeping a steady mind, understanding the situation, and choosing where to chanelize your strength.

I think I don’t have to edit my story right away, I don’t even think I need to abandon it and start a new one, maybe I just need to accept that its bad and I even need to understand the characters, the story, the plot more and find the first easiest mistake, maybe that tempts me to edit? I see that as a plan, I can see that doable and I can see myself completing it successfully. Thats’s a mindful thinking aloud. You are not alone, just tap in your soul, you’ll find voices to help you.

Perhaps day 3 is rightly about worshipping Maa Chandra Ghanta and what a beautiful combination of it. She is the one that provides confidence and the will power to rise against fear and ego. Its not about merely the courage to confront our fears, but the wisdom to do so without letting those fears control our actions.  
  
She represents bravery, grace, and the fierce side of the Goddess, alongside her calm, gentle nature. She is associated with the Manipura (solar plexus) chakra, linked to confidence, willpower, and inner strength. I have discussed and written about it in the past in [2025](https://mythstomeaning.substack.com/p/navratri-day-3-balancing-courage) and [2024](https://mythstomeaning.substack.com/p/navratri-day-3-2024-maa-chandraghanta), do read through to get more confidence.

## Conclusion

Perhaps the third choice is not about finding a way to avoid the difficult decision. I think it is about approaching that decision a bit differently. I can’t have to publish my first novel to justify the six months I worked on writing it. I don’t have to abandon it to prove that I can write something better than it.

I have the power to take a step back, understand what I’ve written or created or done, and figure out what needs to be done to them. After that I’ll discover that the story needs a lot of editing, or maybe I will realise that its better as a short story, or maybe I will learn enough to begin a new story with a better understanding of my writing and interests.

I don’t know yet what becomes of that story, that is still in my control yes? And that is powerful enough to push me through the fear and uncertainty.

I think sometimes that’s what courage means in some quote right? not the absence of fear, but the ability to think clearly and move and work through despite of it.

On this third day of Navratri, Maa Chandraghanta reminds me of the strength I want to cultivate, that is the courage to face what frightens me, and the composure to decide how to face it.

I still have a novel to figure out. But at least I have a place to begin.

Jay Bhavani! Jay Ambe!
