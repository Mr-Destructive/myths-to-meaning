Here I am, day 9 of writing this. When habits become in-grained, nothing stops you from doing it, not even the mind can resist it, the mind makes it normal after a while. Such is the power of habit, very easy to create, and easy to remove too. That was me writing this 9 day posts for 3 consecutive years. I don’t have to think or question myself why I am doing it, I just do it. Even though this year it was more philosophical than mythological it seemed, but it was important for me to get out of the shell.

The last day, is not the last however, it means we have to reflect on what we have done so far. The cycle will continue, because no one’s perfect and nobody can be, we can be a little better from yesterday, or last year, or last birth? Who knows, let’s control what we can, and forget the uncontrollable.

## Introduction

We have learnt a lot of lessons, like acknowledgement of our flaws, seeing through the ego and fear with our pre-defined ways of doing actions, creating responsibly without attachment, putting purpose over pleasure, removing bad habits, detaching the mind’s image from self, and now we need to connect the dots. It seems daunting right?

## Connecting the dots

Well, it can be if you have stumbled at some steps, nobody is perfect, the goal is not be perfect right? But to be better. So even if you feel overwhelmed, just think it what is the deeper meaning behind your action and the consequences till now.

For me, I wrote a novel, spent 6 months writing it, understood that its not perfect, the writing is purple prose with a lot of fluff, but its beautiful descriptive, I know that I am not a writer or author yet, but I am trying to become one, I honestly have tried to write my heart out. And that is a win for me. I have stepped over my fear and ego and created something that I can admire, but I realised that I don’t need to stop here, I can make it better, I can bring it to life, I can make someone happy if they really read my story but after a few improvements. I have tried to remove the impression that the story is perfect or the way it is supposed to be, because the first draft is exactly that. You tell the story to yourself, get the words out. Because you can’t tell the story and make it readable at the same time.

The process of writing is something I learnt, not fully, never will. But I realised I emit my emotions, my state of mind in my writing, that is powerful but not always, I don’t have the control on my flow yet, I think that comes over time right? Maybe it does, but that’s a strong signal to follow my instinct and make it better.

Just like that we need to think about the lessons we have learnt, what we intended, what is the current situation, what we truly want from ourselves, not our materialistic goal, but from a true sense of satisfaction purpose. That helps us connect with the nature and work in harmony rather than fighting with your soul and mind.

## Conclusion

That cannot be a beautiful conclusion then appreciating Maa Siddhi Datri on the ninth day of Navratri. The word itself says, the one who blesses or gives / donates the siddhis or supernatural powers/accomplishments. The ability to be humble, strength to rise, ability to express emotions, ability to help, kindness or compassion, intelligence and materialistic desires, control over self and the surrounding. You can debate about these qualities as super powers but they are if you have control over your senses and you can use them correctly. I know there is a different meaning of them as I have myself explored in the past [2025](https://mythstomeaning.substack.com/p/navratri-day-9-circling-back-and) and [2024](https://mythstomeaning.substack.com/p/navratri-day-9-2024-maa-siddhidatri) years of writing this.

If you can control these and use them righteously then you have connected the dots, I know I can’t use all at right times, but even if I use 5/10 times right, I am much better then failing all the times right? We can always try, you know
