In the modern world, I think evil is not outside, it's inside our body, the mind. It plays quite tricky thoughts on us, such that we are left helpless but to indulge in those thoughts and kind of get blackmailed from it. This thought that the mind a evil counterplay on us is powerful, because without your actions, the mind is also helpless. But the important part is to know when to listen and when to ignore.

## Introduction

When we are doing some physical activities like daily chores, or helping someone, or any work, our mind is not doing it right? Our physical hands and muscles are doing the hard work, but still sometimes our lazy mind urges us to stop it, when nothing is making it consume energy.

Similarly, there are masks that our mind puts on our eyes or our senses, which are fear, anxiety, anger, greed, lust, and others that delude us from reality.

## The tricks of the mind

The mind is the mastermind of playing stories and inferring things in our head, but not all things can be taken as they are, we can’t act impulsively on all thoughts produced by it. The mind can be a devil if all the thoughts are acted by you without question or righteous thinking, but it can be a best friend when your intellect and energy are devoted only to righteous thoughts.

## Being conscious and aware

The mind can throw at us thousands of thoughts each day, we can’t act on all. We can’t fall for all of it. We have to understand which thought will lead us to the right direction. Take for example, you are new in a city, you want to go from A to B, you stand at a railway station, at first you don’t know where is B train coming, so you pick up the train at random, you end at the wrong station, but then over time, with mistakes and error, we know that this train leads to C and then we can decide whether to board in that train or not right?

You have to be aware that the thought the mind has given to you is in your righteous interest or not, if yes then act on it, but if not then ignore it.

## Rising above the evil

If the mind is the evil actor in our body, we need to take right steps and without fear or doubt slain those thoughts out of our way, if by simple ignarnace and humility the thoughts are not vanishing, its best to take strict actions.

This is the lesson we can learn from Maa Kaalratri, who when evil demons don’t realise their mistakes after soft plea from Maa Paarvati, she takes a vicious form to slain the demons of Chanda/Munda and Raktabeej. When evil is too powerful, you too have to stand and face it. That’s the strength Maa Kaalratri gives us to face the evil without fear and with confidence.

## Conclusion

Its the seventh day of Navratri, and we have gone from accessing our actions to controlling our pleasures, today, we need to take actions if the inner thoughts or bad habits are not getting erased. That is why we take the blessings of Maa Kaalratri, or Maa Kaali to give us the strength and confidence to stand up against such thoughts and actions that lead us astray in our righteous or intended path.

May Maa Kaalratri bless you with all the strength and evict the bad habits and thoughts from your life.

Jay Bhavani ! Jay Ambe!
