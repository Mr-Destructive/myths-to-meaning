We all want to be a better person, but when we try, something stops us, or should I say, we ourselves think we can’t be that person because we are someone else. And that is a bit of delusional thinking, isn’t it?

We are also confused about whether “to become a better person” or to “see ourselves as a better person”, one is for others, the other is for our ego. If we have to truly become a better person, no matter what it takes, we have to dissolve the current perception of ourselves. That is the question for the eighth day of Navratri, when Maa Mahagauri would help us become pure not from outside but from inside.

## Introduction

We have moved from understanding our flaws, dealing with fear, ego, and negative traits, and even understood that we need to take action on negative emotions when they get into our head. But what about the thoughts or preconceived images that we have in our head that we keep telling ourselves? I would say that is not evil, but it makes us stagnate or not be true to our potential.

When I say I want to be an author, my mind assumes an image of a perfect author, and when I try to write something, it just doesn’t match the expectation, and I go into a downward spiral of imposter syndrome and all sorts of procrastination to avoid facing it.

## The trap of self-image

Our mind is really powerful in imagining things, and the moment you desire something it already starts to imagine it. Without knowing if you really want it or need it or not. The image sticks with us, and we start to act on it, we realize we are too bad at it, or sometimes too good at it. That makes it very hard to get over ourselves, either with imposter syndrome or overconfidence. We trap oursleves into a image that is not our true image but nonetheless we keep believing it.

Until a point when everything looks normal and we accept it as our reality, we start living with that image and it becomes normal. And that is a bigger trap then being evil. Because your true potential is trapped in your soul.

## Detaching the image

I think it’s quite hard to detach from that image rather than facing evil emotions. The hard part is that our mind tells us we are “already that” what we want, we start to live with that. We assume we are good, we are the vision that we hold in our head, which we actually have actually abandoned the moment our mind created a different image. The separation of truth and reality hits too hard when it hits, and the mind plays a deeper game in keeping us with the image.

Detaching from the image, the preconceived image that we have of our vision or dreams, need to be done, because we can’t attach our actions to our goals. If we start doing that, it might again lead to the trap of ego and the full spiral of destruction.

We have to accept that our mind won’t be able to erase that image from our head, rather we have to let it go. We have to keep on learning, we need to keep our thinking like a child, always curious and that will surely distract our mind from that old image. It can re-kindle a hope, a spark that the soul needs. A hope is something the universe is living on. We don’t have to reject our image, that might backfire and lead to negative actions. Instead we have to channelise the energy and our thinking in other direction or the true direction we want in order to see the difference.

The question to ask is if you stopped believing in the person you believe you are, who else you will allow yourself to become. And that question is not rejecting the person or the image the mind has created, rather its a genuine inquiry or a reality check for our mind to re-think or re-imagine the image.

Let your actions reveal who you are becoming, rather than forcing your actions to prove who you think you are right?

## Conclusion

On the eight day of Navratri, we carry a subtle yet important and quite tricky path to navigate which is to detach ourselves from the false image of ourselves that our mind tricks into believing.

Maa Maha Gauri teaches us the those principles that we need to purify ourselves from the cluttered image we have of ourselves. Simplicity is the key, we can’t fake or dress ourselves in other’s reality where we have different situations. The transformation is possible no matter how badly your mind has trapped you, there is redemption, that’s how maha gauri emerged from the holy water of Ganga, all her impurity was removed, and that was not just due to the holy water but also of her penance and sacrifice of hundreds and thousands of years, surrender without effort is not really a surrender, its laziness.
