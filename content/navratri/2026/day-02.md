If first day was about being delusional, the second day is about going deeper on it. I will continue my examples (sorry if it sounds cringe, but I like to stick to one example and explain it in different angles or flaws) of me writing the novel last year.

## Introduction

I wrote the novel in the span of 6 months, but then abandoned it once it was completed. It has taken me another year to realise I have been ignorant about my writing. I never took that piece of writing and try to re-write it, I asked ai to critique it, and the task seemed a long list of improvements, including major structural changes and flipping the story inside out. On top of it, AI was just slaughtering the world with its slop, I was afraid that what becomes of true writing. I hate to read text written by AI, but the line quickly blurred on what was AI and what was human.

That all aside, the point is how quickly I accepted that writing was worthless in near future, so I should not waste time in writing stories. “What an idiot!” I am. If you know the line reference, you know. Having that thought was one thing, but deciding it that quickly was one heck of thing that ruined my chemistry of satisfaction. I love writing, but something held me back, or I let it.

## The haste to ignorance

It took me six months to write a novel, and it took me one afternoon to decide it didn’t matter. Not saying the decision was not taken at once, but over time it kept validating itself with AI and excuses in life to not look at it. There was a haste in ignoring it. The confrontation was delayed as quickly as it was ignored right? Because, ignoring it immediately is much easier than facing the flaws of our selves.

The same story is learnt from Mahishasura here. Mahishasura never fought with fair means. He kept changing shape, buffalo, then lion, then something else, because a demon who can’t be pinned down can’t be beaten, nobody knows its strength and weakness, if it keeps changing. I was doing the same thing but with my excuses. It was never really about the critique or the AI. I just created more excuses that reasons to write or edit the novel.

There was ignorance as destination, and haste was its vehicle. I am not saying haste is bad, infact, speedy actions most of the times are better than no actions, laziness is not good, but avoiding something and doing some other activity just to postpone the action is not meaningful.

## Unperturbed Focus

I think, If I really wanted to edit the novel I could have done it, but there is a hastiness combined with laziness that avoided it. But if I was focussed on the goal, I would have done, just like the first draft was done. But change is something that makes us resist the action.

I think this lesson will help me become a better writer. I can write for hours, but there are distractions, there is FOMO, there is AI. Every thing just goes against my focus, fights for my attention and I swindle in between. With AI, it has made us more scattered and switch context too frequently. I think and I hope, writing becomes one of the skills that keeps our focus in check, otherwise, all our skills are atrophying. Unperturbed focus, or deep focus as someone calls it is going to be a rare skill if no one slows down.

With that, the second day of Navratri is dedicated to Maa Brahmacharini, the goddess of penance. I have written on it in the past 2 years, in [2025](https://mythstomeaning.substack.com/p/navratri-day-2-focus-with-nature) and [2024](https://mythstomeaning.substack.com/p/navratri-day-2-2024-dwitiya-worshipping) too. The story goes that she meditated for thousands of years, with no promise that her devotion would be rewarded. She didn’t ask whether it was worth it. The effort itself was the devotion.

Its like the opposite of haste right? Its not about avoiding actions, but being more conscious of what the action and the decision we take to continue or abandon the action. This decision cannot be taken correctly without having deep connection with yourself, what you like or dislike, what you want and need, what is your duty and what is not. Otherwise, there will be countless other things fighting for your attention and focus.

## Conclusion

That is great combination of learning for day 2, knowing the flaws of our mind is one part and then the other part is silencing the inner lazy or hasty evil voice that avoid the actions that gives me inner satisfaction.

With that I end todays learning and get on to writing some essays and short stories, its still hard to start but getting into motion is better than staying in delusion and ignorance.

Jay Bhavani ! Jay Ambe !
