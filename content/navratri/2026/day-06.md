## Introduction

We all have desires to live in a certain way, do a certain thing, get a certain thing, etc. But the intention is what gets polluted in achieving that. We love to create, nurture somethings, without attaching ourselves on it right, but what happens when we start indulging in the pleasure of the action rather then finding the purpose of doing it and sustainably doing it.

## Pleasure as the false target

If someone asked me, why are you a software developer, I would answer like “because I love solving problems”, there it is, a pleasure of my mind. But if someone asks me a deeper question, “what is the purpose of solving problems?”, then I might dwindle. Because purpose and pleasure are something that have gotten blurred. Your purpose cannot be “I **love** to solve problems”, the love part is a attachment, it’s your ego speaking that I do it because I love it, not for anyone else. Even though your intention might be pure, the ego in the mind takes over and the purpose is lost. Because your true purpose is not “to solve problems”, it’s to “help people simplify their problems”. The pleasure is what gets in between that fine line of “I love this” to “to help people”, the moment you attach “I” in your purpose, that is “pleasure”.

I am not saying to abandon your pleasure, do it, but keep your purpose aligned and don’t let the ego in the mind take over.

## Purpose as the aim

It’s sometimes or rather in modern day 2026, quite challenging to find your purpose it seems right? People have lost the ability to calm their over-stimulated mind and think for themselves. The mind is deluded by the ego to take the easiest path which is to say “pleasure as my purpose” or “what you truly love is your purpose”. That can be true, but only to get a hint and not to just blindly doing it.

We need to find the intersection of our pleasure and benefit of others too right?

Otherwise we can become over-indulgent in our pleasures, or too bored with the things that we don’t like. Extremes are really hurting in this case, find the middle ground. If you love building and seeing things automated, software is a profession you can walk into. If you like to day-dream and seeing people resonate and motivated, writing might be for you, if you like to serve people and have the thrill, you can be a doctor or nurse. There are endless options in this world. The problem is today we are overwhelmed with the options and it feels nothing is right for us.

Its just about finding your purpose, you know. It might take you journaling, solitude, or even conversations and observations to connect the dots.

## Conclusion

On the sixth day of navratri we worship Maa Katyayani who symbolizes the fierce energy needed to conquer internal demons, represented by Mahishasura. Her story highlights the transformative journey of self-awareness and inner battle against one's own karmas. I have written about it in the previous year in [2025](https://mythstomeaning.substack.com/p/navratri-day-6-unleashing-the-potential) and [2024](https://mythstomeaning.substack.com/p/navratri-day-6-2024-maa-katyayani), do read it to explore more, its quite interesting.

We can connect that to our struggle to find the purpose, its a battle actually right? A inner-battle to not let find our purpose and resist the pleasure to take control completely. I hope and pray that you find your balance in finding your purpose and pleasure soon.
