The **Shiv Tandav Stotram** is a powerful hymn dedicated to Lord Shiva, celebrating his cosmic dance, the Tandava. Traditionally attributed to Ravana, it expresses awe for Shiva's divine qualities, strength, and the profound energy of his dance, which symbolises creation, preservation and destruction.

Composed of 17 verses, each is explored here literally, mythologically, and spiritually. Below you'll find every verse published in this series.
