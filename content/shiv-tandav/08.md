## Shiv Tandav Stotram

The [Shiv Tandav Stotram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiva_Tandava_Stotra) is a powerful hymn dedicated to Lord Shiva, celebrating his cosmic dance, the Tandava. It is suspected to be composed by Ravana (not proven though), and it expresses awe for Shiva’s divine qualities, strength, and the profound energy of his dance, which symbolizes creation, preservation, and destruction. The storm serves as a prayer for spiritual awakening, invoking blessings of purity, power, and auspiciousness.

There are a total of [17 verses](https://hindi.webdunia.com/aarti-chalisa/shiv-tandav-stotram-114100100059_1.html) in the stotram. We will be exploring each verse separately and understanding its interpretation literally, mythologically as well as spiritually.

However, people might call it a destructive act but as with anything in the world, it depends on how you perceive it. If you take it positively it is a great source of inspiration and a heartful praise sung by the author (whoever it may be).

## Verse 8

> **नवीनमेघमंडली निरुद्धदुर्धरस्फुर**
>
> **त्कुहुनिशीथनीतमः प्रबद्धबद्धकन्धरः।**
>
> **निलिम्पनिर्झरीधरस्तनोतु कृत्तिसिंधुरः**
>
> **कलानिधानबंधुरः श्रियं जगंद्धुरंधरः ॥**

> Naveena-Megha-Mandali Nirudha-Durdhara-Sphura
>
> T-Kuhu-Nishitha-Nitamaha Prabadha-badha-Kandharaha
>
> Nilimpa-Nirjari-Dharasta-Notu Kruti-Sindhuraha
>
> Kalaa-Nidhaan-Bandhuraha Shriyam Jagad-dhurarundharaha

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Meaning

One whose presence is like a new cloud formation that is restrained yet radiantly powerful, whose neck is surrounded by deep, enveloping darkness like a night.

Who bears the divine river (Ganges) on his head and is adorned with animal skin, symbolizing his ascetic nature and his role as a divine protector, embodying all arts and knowledge, sustaining the world’s prosperity and beauty. He is likely a deity who harmonizes cosmic and earthly elements.

## Word by Word Meaning

> नवीनमेघमंडली निरुद्धदुर्धरस्फुर
>
> त्कुहुनिशीथनीतमः प्रबद्धबद्धकन्धरः

> Naveena-Megha-Mandali Nirudha-Durdhara-Sphura
>
> T-Kuhu-Nishi-Tha-Ni-Tamaha Prabadha-badha-Kandharaha

One whose presence is like a new cloud formation that is restrained yet radiantly powerful, whose neck is surrounded by deep, enveloping darkness like a night.

- **नवीनमेघमंडली (Navina-Megha-Mandali): A collection of new clouds**

  - नवीन (Navina): New or fresh

  - मेघ (Megha): Cloud

  - मंडली (Mandali): Collection, assembly, or group

- **निरुद्धदुर्धरस्फुर (Niruddha-Duradhara-Sphurat): Restrained but powerful, sparkling, and radiant**

  - निरुद्ध (Niruddha): Restrained or contained

  - दुर्धर (Durdhara): Difficult to restrain, powerful

  - स्फुर (Sphurat): Radiant, sparkling, or pulsating

- **त्कुहुनिशीथनीतमः (Kuhu-Nishitaha-Nitamaha): The intense darkness of a new moon night**

  - कुहुनिशीथ (Kuhu-Nishitaha): Refers to the new moon night or the darkness of an Amavasya night

  - नीतमः (Nitamaha): Extreme or intense darkness

- **प्रबद्धबद्धकन्धरः (Parabaddha-Baddha-Kandharaha):** **Firmly bound at the neck**

  - प्रबद्ध (Prabaddha): Firmly bound

  - बद्ध (Baddha): Tied or bound

  - कन्धरः (Kadharaha): Neck or throat.

> निलिम्पनिर्झरीधरस्तनोतु कृत्तिसिंधुरः
>
> कलानिधानबंधुरः श्रियं जगंद्धुरंधरः

> Nilimpa-Nirjari-Dharasta-Notu Kruti-Sindhuraha
>
> Kalaa-Nidhaan-Bandhuraha Shriyam Jagad-dhurarundharaha

Lord Shiva, who bears the divine river (Ganges) on his head and is adorned with animal skin, symbolizing his ascetic nature and his role as a divine protector, embodies all arts and knowledge, sustaining the world’s prosperity and beauty. He is likely a deity who harmonizes cosmic and earthly elements.

- **निलिम्पनिर्झरीधरस्तनोतु (Nilimpa-Nirjhari-Dharasta-Tanotu): Bearer of the celestial stream, likely referring to the Ganges River, which flows from Shiva’s matted hair.**

  - निलिम्प (Nilimpa): Refers to celestial beings, often taken to mean gods or divine beings

  - निर्झरी (Nirjari): Stream or waterfall

  - धर (Dhara): Bearer or one who holds

  - तनोतु (Tanotu): May (he) bestow

- **कृत्तिसिंधुरः (Kruti-Sindhuraha): One adorned with a hide, possibly indicating a warrior or ascetic bearing a tiger or animal skin.**

  - कृत्ति (Kruti): Hide or skin, often referring to an animal hide

  - सिंधुरः (Sindhuraha): Marked or adorned with.

- **कलानिधानबंधुरः (Kalani-Dhana-Bandhuraha): The charming one who is a repository of knowledge or the moon’s phases**

  - कलानिधान (Kalaa-Nidhaana): Repository of arts, knowledge, or phases (often referring to the moon's phases)

  - बंधुरः (Bandhuraha): Graceful, charming.

- **श्रियं (Shriyam): Prosperity, beauty, or grace**

- **जगंद्धुरंधरः (Jagad-Durandharaha): The one who upholds the beauty and prosperity of the universe**

  - जगत् (Jagat): World or universe

  - धुरंधरः (Dhurandharaha): One who upholds or sustains

## Mythological Meaning

The verse describes Shiva as a cosmic power, restrained yet formidable. He is connected to the mystery and depth of the dark night and bears the symbols of asceticism and self-control.

This verse highlights Shiva’s role as a compassionate deity who balances cosmic forces. His act of holding the Ganges on his head, his ascetic appearance, his mastery over knowledge and arts, and his role in sustaining the universe’s prosperity, all underscore his function as both a fierce ascetic and a caring protector of the cosmos.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Spiritual Meaning

- **Transcendence from Darkness to Light**: The description of Shiva’s neck (as throat chakra) piercing through the moon’s darkness represents the soul’s journey from ignorance to enlightenment. This gives us a message that however the dark times may be, faith and inner girt and divine grace can cut through it.

- **Spreading Creativity and Love**: The mention of Lord Shiva as the universal bearer, indicates how loving and caring is he for the universe, the act of charity and blessing with grace shows his pure intentions. Likewise, we can cultivate the practice of giving out to others selflessly without thinking about the benefits for us.
